Your terminal.
Their browser.

A deploy can take minutes. Nobody should have to wait for a screenshot. Pipe your terminal output to shpellcast and share a link your team can watch live.

Append to a command in your terminal

| nc shpellcast.godtech.in 9696
▷ See it in action Try a sample ↓

Use the nc or curl you already have. No shpellcast install or account.

Cast your first line.

Run the sample, then open the link that prints in your terminal. Send it to whoever needs to follow along.

Anyone with the link can watch until it expires. Keep secrets and sensitive output out of public casts.

printf 'Hello from my terminal\n' | nc shpellcast.godtech.in 9696

The nc pipe uses plain TCP. If it stays open after the command finishes, press Ctrl+C.

Use curl over HTTPS
printf 'Hello from my terminal\n' | curl -fsSN -T - https://shpellcast.godtech.in/ingest
